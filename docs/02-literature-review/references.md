# References (IEEE)

Add each source here **after** you have read it and checked its details
against the actual paper. Tick the box when verified. Final numbering is in
order of **first citation** in the document, not alphabetical, and is fixed
when the chapters are assembled.

Format (guideline 11.11): initials first; article titles in sentence case
inside quotes; IEEE-abbreviated journal names in italics; page ranges with an
en dash (pp. 369–376); access dates as (accessed Mon. DD, YYYY).

Patterns:
- Journal: A. A. Author, "Title of article," *Abbrev. Title of Journal*, vol. x, no. x, pp. xxx–xxx, year, doi: xxxxx.
- Conference: A. A. Author, "Title of paper," in *Proc. Name of Conference*, City, Country, year, pp. xxx–xxx.
- Book: A. A. Author, *Title of Book*, xth ed. City, Country: Publisher, year.
- Web page: A. A. Author or Organization, "Title of page," Website Name. [Online]. Available: URL (accessed Mon. DD, YYYY).
- Software or dataset: A. A. Author or Organization, "Title," version x.x, year. [Online]. Available: URL or DOI (accessed Mon. DD, YYYY).
