# Change log (Section 4.6)

A change is **major** if it alters the problem, the general objective, any
specific objective, the primary dataset or technology, the group membership or
the advisor, or delays a milestone by more than two weeks. A major change
needs a written request with the reason and the effect on scope, schedule and
testing, endorsed by the advisor and approved by the Committee **before**
the work expands. Minor wording changes need the advisor's consent. The
project title can only change before the FYP I defense.
